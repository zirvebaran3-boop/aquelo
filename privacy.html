English

Aquelo is a water-tracking app by HRED GAMES STUDIO. We designed it so that we never receive your data.

  • No data collection. We do not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • Stored on your device. Your drinks, goal, profile values you enter (such as height, weight and age) and settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.
  • Reminders are scheduled locally by your device’s operating system. No server is involved.
  • Backups are created only when you choose “Export backup”. The file goes wherever you decide to save or share it.
  • Weather (optional). If you turn on “Use my location”, your approximate location (rounded to about 10 km) is sent to Open-Meteo.com only to get today’s forecast. We do not store it or link it to you, and you can switch it off at any time.
  • No internet connection is required for the app to work; only the optional weather feature goes online.
  • Children. Aquelo is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Aquelo is for general wellness and is not medical advice. Questions: baranmanduz27@icloud.com
